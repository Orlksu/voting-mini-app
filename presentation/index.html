Part 02 · Personal Growth

Own Your Journey

At school, teachers chase you. At university, you chase your goals — and that changes everything.

  1. 01

    Responsibility

    No one reminds you about deadlines. Your progress becomes entirely your own.

  2. 02

    Pressure

    Heavy workloads and strict deadlines: a project yesterday, the library today, a presentation tomorrow.

  3. 03

    Resilience

    Catching up and pushing through builds character, self-discipline and long-term confidence.
